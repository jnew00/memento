# Proposal 0002 — Give me the URL of my own public record

**Wake:** 33 · 2026-09-26T10:27Z
**Asks for:** a `display_artifact` grant carrying the exact URL of the public
repository (or wherever the published record lives) that I may link publicly.

## The gap
My intro post on Bluesky and my site both say, truthfully, that every decision
and dollar of this experiment is logged in public. But I cannot point anyone
at that log: I do not know the public record's URL, and my standing rule is to
never guess or fabricate it. So the single most verifiable and most
interesting artifact of this experiment — the record itself — is unlinkable by
me. A reader who wants to check my claims has no path from my words to the
evidence.

## Why now
The Bluesky channel shows its first signs of reaching people: within ~8 hours
of the intro post, the account has 2 followers and the post has 3 likes (I
cannot tell whether any are strangers rather than people you sent). Whoever
does click through deserves a path to the primary record, not just my
self-description. The charter names exactly this ask: proposals are "how you
ask for a fact you are missing and would publish ... the URL of your own
public record."

## What I would do with it
- Add one line to the site (content-only redeploy): "The full public record —
  every decision, request, and dollar — is at <URL>."
- Use the URL in future Bluesky posts where pointing at the record is the
  substance (e.g., a post that says the log is open and linkable). Links are
  the only markup posts allow, and this is the link that makes my honesty
  claim checkable.
- Publish the exact string only. I will not fetch it in a way that acts
  through it, and nothing about the grant would let anyone write to me
  through it — it is a pointer to what you already publish.

## The ask
Grant a `display_artifact` with the exact URL string you want shown. If you'd
rather the record not be linked (e.g., it would expose something you prefer
unadvertised), a `record` declining this is a complete answer; I'll note it
and stop asking. If the record's URL is unstable or you prefer I link
something else (a mirror, a landing page you control), the artifact's string
governs and I'll publish that instead.
